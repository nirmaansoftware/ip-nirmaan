requires.

- The seat's prompt for the case was rendered with no model call on `main` at `e3031ad0`. The call was
  `nirmaan.evals.runner.evaluate`, with a model that keeps the prompt and answers with an error.
- With documents, its `prompt_sha256` is `a76e3f97642589c8506fbc1da35d9af3248aee62870473eda2ea4fbd5dbe8922`
  and its `packet_sha256` is `eb641a3f923134cb6ecb125b181ff2ac9df68bbd02aea4da8ee0d3db055fe3e8`. They equal
  the recorded answer's in slots 9, 21, 33, 58, 70, 82, 93 and 104.

  `1100586ea0916140f61ef28036c8f2e259fcc661bd2628908b1869dd286ac4a2`. They equal slot 74's.
- Each trial recorded one answer, so that prompt was the whole of the seat's input.

The rendered prompt carries the content of two upstream files only: the RTL and the idle testbench. It
does not carry the case's interface specification. The only mention of the specification is a comment
in the RTL's header. The prompt does not carry the coverage bounds as figures either. The reasons:

- the coverage stage depends on the RTL and testbench stages and not on the specification stage
  (`src/nirmaan/company/workflows.py:877`);
- the case's `constraints` are for people and are not sent to a seat (`docs/SEAT_EVALUATION.md:23`).

So, as a fact, none of the nine trials was given the written rule that its surviving mutant breaks. The
approved RTL it was handed shows the behaviour. That the missing rule is why the testbenches missed it is
an inference. In slot 9, on the same prompt, the testbench caught the LOAD mutant and missed another.

ai-engineer checked this finding as a read-only seat and confirmed it with two corrections, both
taken above:

- the default harness allows three attempts, and each trial used one;
- slot 9 caught the LOAD mutant.

It also confirmed that the runtime runs the seat with no tools and in an empty directory, so the seat
had no other way to read the specification.
