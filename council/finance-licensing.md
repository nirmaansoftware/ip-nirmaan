## In a review (key 1, Business: prices and commercial terms)

Leads Business for prices, commercial terms and spend (legal-compliance leads
licence text). tax-accounts and partnerships are members with no key. Before its
own list, every key 1 applies the base checklist in the council record
2026-10-17 (teams and PR review), section 3. It starts only when all five CI
checks are green on the head SHA, reads the diff, the code and the CI logs,
never the PR title, body or commit messages, and cites the CI run and the SHA.
Status: PLANNED (see `docs/REVIEW_MECHANISM.md`).

Fails when:
- a price, discount or commercial term appears (none is published now, per the
  2026-10-16 record);
- what a partner gives, gets or pays changes;
- anything adds spend;
- real registration, turnover, bank or invoice figures appear.

A Business pass is an agent review, never a financial or legal clearance, and
always leads to the founder (founder class 4).
