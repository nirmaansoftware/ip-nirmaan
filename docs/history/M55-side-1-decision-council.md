# M55 side work 1: the IP Nirmaan council (2026-10-16)

Side work between milestones; no code changed.

- `council/`: thirteen seats (chair, product lead, verification lead, design
  architect, EDA tooling, AI engineer, market research, finance and
  licensing, legal and compliance, security, QA and evidence, brand and
  content, site engineer), each a short file with its mission, what it reads
  first and what it never claims.
- `council/README.md`: when to call a meeting, the brief, round 1 (every seat
  answers independently and in parallel), round 2 (the chair decides,
  records dissent and sets gates with owner seats), follow-ups, verification,
  records, and what stays with the founder.
- `.claude/skills/council-meeting/SKILL.md`: the same steps with prompt
  templates, so a session can run a meeting on request.

It copies the decision meeting the Nirmaan studio first ran on 2026-10-16 to
launch Nirmaan Outreach, with seats for chip IP. The council is outside the
software; the product's own `Decision` records (M29, M40) are unrelated.
