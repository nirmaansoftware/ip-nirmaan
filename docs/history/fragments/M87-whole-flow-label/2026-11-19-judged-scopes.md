**Judged scopes.** A confirm check after the fifth check's splits found new escapes, each at a value the testbench copies never try: `AXIL-B2B` after a `READY` wait of 1 or 3 to 6 cycles, `TMR-STOP-EDGE` at untested `LOAD` values, `TMR-START` restarts at untested `COUNT` positions. Since any finite testbench can be escaped that way, every judged tag of both flow cases now carries its stimulus scope under `judged_scope` in its `citations.json` (the values, waits, positions, widths and orderings the case's checks exercise for it, with line references), and a judged tag is judged within that scope: a block that breaks a rule only outside it is not an escape. Each requirements document says so in its opening paragraph. `TMR-ONESHOT-VALUES` and `TMR-PERIODIC-VALUES` are gone; their `LOAD` lists are now the scopes of `TMR-COUNT-LENGTH`, `TMR-ONESHOT` and `TMR-PERIODIC`. `tests/test_whole_flow_cases.py` requires a non-empty scope citing a line for every judged tag, and a check record now carries a copy of the scopes it checked within, so a changed scope makes it stale. The testbench copies, the mutant sweep records and the fingerprints are unchanged; both documents wait for a fresh check.
