Semiconductor IP · Early stage

Chip IP, built as software.

IP Nirmaan is an AI-native semiconductor IP company modeled as software: you give it a requirement, and a machine-readable organization plans, routes, reviews, and gates the work, with evidence behind every claim.

Talk to us How it works ↓

Real output

One sentence in. A whole plan out.

What nirmaan plan prints for a 4-port AXI-to-NoC bridge, trimmed. Every task has an owner, and every gate names the human who approves it.

The idea

Engineering work, run by an organization you can read.

Three rules the whole system is built on.

  • An organization, not a chatbot.

    Divisions, teams, and roles from intern to CEO, each with skills, authority, and an escalation path.

  • Planned is not done.

    Work moves through planned, executed, verified, and approved, and nothing skips a step.

  • Evidence or it did not happen.

    A tool run, a review, or a named person's sign-off backs every claim. An AI cannot mark its own work approved.

How it works

From one sentence to routed, gated work.

Follow one requirement through the organization. Scroll to move it along.

  1. Requirement

    A plain-language request comes in. Nothing else is needed to start.

  2. Analyze

    Intent, features, and parameters are extracted. Anything left unstated becomes a written assumption, not a silent guess.

  3. Route to roles

    Each task goes to the role whose skills, capabilities, and authority fit it. Routing reads the organization as data.

  4. Review and gate

    Work is executed, then verified by an independent reviewer. Gates such as architecture approval wait for a named human.

  5. Deliverable

    The work product arrives with its trail: who did what, from which inputs, backed by which evidence.

The company constitution

Twelve principles the engine enforces.

Each one is a blocking check: an action that breaks it is refused, and the refusal is written to the audit trail.

  1. P1

    Traceability

    Every task traces to the requirement it serves.

  2. P2

    Evidence for decisions

    Important engineering decisions require evidence.

  3. P3

    Explicit uncertainty

    Agents must explicitly represent uncertainty.

  4. P4

    No fabricated work

    Agents must not fabricate completed work.

  5. P5

    No fabricated tool runs

    No claim that a tool ran unless it did.

  6. P6

    Independent review

    Critical work is reviewed. Nobody approves their own work.

  7. P7

    Conflicts escalate

    Conflicting outputs are escalated before approval.

  8. P8

    Provenance

    Every change records who, what, and from which inputs.

  9. P9

    Evidence for signoff

    Signoff requires evidence.

  10. P10

    No hidden state changes

    Every change goes through the engine and the audit trail.

  11. P11

    Auditability

    The audit trail is append-only and hash-chained.

  12. P12

    Human approval at gates

    Gates can require a human to approve.

Built on VeriTriage

The verification engine inside.

VeriTriage turns simulation logs, coverage, and waveforms into an evidence graph and a ranked, evidence-backed root cause. When IP Nirmaan triages a regression, VeriTriage does the investigation, and its results become evidence in the task.

Status and roadmap

What works today, what comes next, and what has not happened yet.

Working today
